Smart matchmaking

Fair rotations, one tap per court

When a court opens, Generate Match picks the fairest four players and pairing, and you can review or edit it before play starts.

  • Prioritizes players by games played and time waited
  • Balanced-skill or social mode, with all three doubles pairings considered
  • Partner locks, rest rules and repeat-opponent limits
  • Breaks, late arrivals and early check-outs stay in the rotation
Import from Reclub

Already on Reclub? Bring your players with you

Paste your Reclub Meet or Competition link and your players are imported automatically, with their ratings. You don’t have to retype names at the front desk.

  • Paste a Reclub event link to pull in the whole player list
  • Prefer files? Paste a list or upload a CSV/TSV roster. Ratings such as DUPR carry over
  • Review every player before anything is added
  • Matching names are flagged so you can skip, link or keep them separate. Nothing is merged automatically
  • Importing the same roster again won’t create duplicates
  • No Reclub login or password needed

Present Courts

Your TV becomes the court board

Open Present Courts on a lobby screen and everyone can see who’s playing where. New lineups get a big on-screen call, so nobody misses their game.

  • Large, read-only display of every court, team and timer
  • Players are called to their court the moment a match is set
  • More than six courts? Pages rotate automatically
Scores & results

Results the moment the last point lands

Record full scores or just the winner. The leaderboard, match history and fairness stats update straight away.

  • Win/loss records, games per player and partner variety
  • Fix a wrong score in seconds; standings recalculate
  • Export results to share or keep for your records
How it works

Up and running before warm-ups end

No hardware, no installs. If you have a laptop, a phone or a TV, you’re set.

Set up your session

Name the session, add courts and pick scoring and matchmaking rules. Paste your Reclub event link to import players, or use a CSV file or type names.

Let players check in

Share the join link or show the QR code. Players join the queue from their phones, and you can still add walk-ins by hand.

Play, rotate, repeat

Generate fair matches as courts open, call them on the TV and record scores. Results and standings take care of themselves.
